ENGINEERS' DAY TRIBUTE

Saluting Bharat Ratna Sir M. Visvesvaraya

Sir M. Visvesvaraya
(1861 – 1962) · Bharat Ratna

Engineers’ Day is celebrated in India on September 15 to honor engineers and their contributions to the nation. The day marks the birth anniversary of Sir M. Visvesvaraya, born in 1861 in Karnataka. A renowned civil engineer and statesman, he contributed to water management, dams, irrigation and flood control, including the KRS Dam and automatic water-control gates. He served as the Dewan of Mysore from 1912–1918 and received the Bharat Ratna in 1955.

Sir M. Visvesvaraya's Life Represents:
Knowledge + Discipline + Innovation + Problem-Solving + Service = Nation Building

Engineers’ Day celebrates his legacy and inspires young engineers to innovate and serve society.
